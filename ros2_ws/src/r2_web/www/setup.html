Every camera plugged into the Pi is listed. Numbers go left to right, like monitors: press Identify and cover a camera with your hand to see which picture goes dark. Direction and field of view apply at once; turning a camera on or off, or renumbering, needs Save and restart.

What R2 sees, from above.
The circle is the dome, the arrow its front. Each fan is a camera: where it points and how wide it sees. Leave a little overlap between neighbours so a face does not get lost passing from one to the next.

R2 seen from above, front up, to scale (rings every 50 cm). Drag a sensor on the map to move it; click it in the list to set where it points, how wide it sees and how far. Red dots are places near R2 that no sensor covers. Pick a test object (top right) and drag it: the sensors that would see it light up, with the distance they would read. Save applies at once: ROS moves the sensors' poses and the lidar's cut.

Each mode starts with a combination on the controller; pressing it again switches the mode off. A combination given to a mode no longer plays its MarcDuino action.
