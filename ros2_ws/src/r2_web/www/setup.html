Console Setup

Every camera plugged into the Pi is listed. Numbers go left to right, like monitors: press Identify and cover a camera with your hand to see which picture goes dark. Direction and field of view apply at once; turning a camera on or off, or renumbering, needs Save and restart.

What R2 sees, from above.
The circle is the dome, the arrow its front. Each fan is a camera: where it points and how wide it sees. Leave a little overlap between neighbours so a face does not get lost passing from one to the next.
